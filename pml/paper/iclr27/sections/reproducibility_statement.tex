\section*{Reproducibility Statement}
The predictive task, direction families, grouped evaluation, and selector
utility are specified in Sections~3--5. Supplementary Sections~S1--S3 give
benchmark construction, inference settings, and the cross-model protocol;
Sections~S4--S7 provide bootstrap, prediction, strength-selection, and endpoint
details. These include computational-environment information and the logged
cohort used for the primary prediction estimates.
