\section{Limitations}

The primary study uses Qwen3-1.7B and two blocks selected on a 500-record
subset. Width-corrected confirmations add Qwen3.5-2B and Ministral-3-3B at
two pre-specified aligned blocks, supporting relative-depth and predictor-order
claims rather than global layer optimality or absolute cross-architecture
incidence comparisons. The Qwen3-1.7B subset differs slightly from the powered
estimate because of sampling and threshold recalibration.

Primary outcomes are teacher-forced margins at two held-out coefficients;
finite-grid topology and free-generation stress tests provide complementary
scope. Schema-constrained probes receive expert audit and exclusion
sensitivity, while independently authored validation remains future work.
Frozen random-control percentiles and the declared utility define the
within-study decision rule; deployment transfer requires recalibration.

The 100-record selector excludes shared fitting and feature-extraction costs,
and neighbor and capability probes cover selected downstream effects. The small
ROME transfer study provides an initial comparison with persistent editing.
