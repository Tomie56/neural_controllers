The cross-model confirmations use 500 frozen records per model and
model-specific random-null thresholds. They establish replication of
within-model contrasts and predictor ordering, but they are not powered for
fine-grained comparisons of absolute incidence across architectures. The
Qwen3-1.7B 500-record block is a subset extraction from the primary outcomes,
so its small deviations from the 3,000-record rates reflect subset composition,
threshold recalibration, and sampling variability rather than a new steering
scale.
